The lane, seen from directly above. Two lane edges holding a constant 22-unit channel through a lane transfer — the manoeuvre a flatbed makes to take a car out of traffic and onto its own protected line. No vanishing point, no illustration: a drafted mark of four strokes that reduces to a favicon and cuts as one-colour vinyl.

One cut, one colour. The same file drives the door, the invoice and the favicon.

TYPEFACE — Manrope (Google Fonts). Full Greek and Latin coverage, geometric skeleton, closed apertures. Wordmark set at 600 with .20em tracking; secondary line at 400 to separate the name from the service.

PALETTE — Graphite #14161A, Paper #FFFFFF, Platinum #C8CCD2. Platinum is a rule-and-detail colour only, never the mark.

SUPERSEDES — perspective road illustration, dashed centre line, EST. 2024, grey gradient.
