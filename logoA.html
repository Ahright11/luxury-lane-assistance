LUXURY LANE ASSISTANCE Identity sheet A Direction — LL monogram Sheet 1 of 1

Two L's, and the lane between them.

The old mark drew the road. This one leaves it empty. One L stands upright, the second is the same L turned 180° — set at a fixed 16-unit remove, so the negative space that separates them cuts a clean diagonal lane through the mark. Nothing illustrated, nothing to lose at small sizes: the letters carry the name, the gap carries the meaning, and the interlock reads as a cradle — the truck holding the car.
